Account & Data Deletion

AppClusterX  •  Effective date: August 2, 2026

Summary: AppClusterX does not have user accounts, sign-in, or a server backend — there is nothing stored anywhere except on your own device. Because of this, there is no "account" to delete, but you can delete all app data at any time using the steps below.

1. No Account System

AppClusterX does not require or offer account creation, sign-in, or sign-up of any kind. You do not create a username/password, link an email, or authenticate with any identity provider to use the app. Since no account exists, there is no account for us to delete, suspend, or deactivate on your behalf.

2. What Data Exists, and Where

The app stores a small amount of data locally on your device only — display name, avatar, game statistics, and app preferences. This data is never uploaded to a server operated by us, and we have no database, backend, or admin system that holds a copy of it. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

3. How to Delete Your Data

Because everything lives on your device, you are always in full control of it. Choose any of the following:

Option A — Reset in-app

  1. Open AppClusterX
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Select Reset Profile

This immediately and permanently clears your display name and avatar from the app.

Option B — Clear app storage (removes everything, including stats and settings)

  1. Open your device's Settings app
  2. Go to Apps (or Apps & notifications)
  3. Select AppClusterX
  4. Tap Storage & cache
  5. Tap Clear storage (or Clear data)

This deletes your profile, game statistics, and preferences, resetting the app to a fresh install state.

Option C — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling AppClusterX from your device removes the app along with all of its locally stored data.

Note on device backups: if Android's automatic backup is enabled on your phone, a copy of this local data may exist in your own Google Account backup until it is next overwritten or you clear it from your Google Account's backup settings. This backup is controlled by Google and your device — we have no access to it and cannot delete it on your behalf.

4. Data We Hold Ourselves

We do not operate any server, database, or analytics platform for this app, so there is no copy of your data held by us to request deletion of. If you believe this is inaccurate or have any related question, contact us using the details below and we will investigate and respond.
